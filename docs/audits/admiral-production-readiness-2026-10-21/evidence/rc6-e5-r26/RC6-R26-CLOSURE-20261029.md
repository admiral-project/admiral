## Security review and residual risks

The R26 security review in [#307](https://github.com/admiral-project/admiral/issues/307#issuecomment-6486471758) records **82/111 PASS, 19 PARTIAL, 10 NOT TESTED, and 0 confirmed FAIL**. No CRITICAL or HIGH vulnerability was confirmed within the executed scope. The remaining controls retain their documented classifications; this closure does not mark them PASS or claim 111/111.

The review records these material residual risks:

- The measured Podman `file` secret driver protects secret files with filesystem permissions; it does not encrypt them at rest. This is a MEDIUM risk for host-storage or offline-snapshot access.
- All workloads share the host account `admiral-apps`. Rootless `UserNS=auto`, SELinux/MCS, namespaces, cgroups, and absent control sockets reduce observed cross-workload access, but do not isolate a compromise of that host account or root.
- Some live negative controls, including direct cross-tenant container administration, remain PARTIAL or NOT TESTED. Their limitations and compensating controls are listed in the review.

The closure is by owner instruction with these residuals visible. It does not assert that all security controls passed or that no vulnerability can exist outside the tested scope.

## Candidate integrity and disposition

No product source, Ansible, installer, RPM spec, or RPM payload changed during final review or this closure. The closure record is documentation only: `PRODUCT_CHANGED=NO`, `CANDIDATE_INVALIDATED=NO`. R26 remains the validated candidate; no rebuild or runtime-cell rerun was required.

PayPal issue #106 remains outside this release gate because it requires external services. AlmaLinux remains outside the matrix due the external COPR tooling limitation.
