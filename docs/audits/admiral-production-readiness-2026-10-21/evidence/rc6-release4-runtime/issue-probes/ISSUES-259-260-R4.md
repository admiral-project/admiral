## #260 — stale availability after a declared PostgreSQL service stops

The issue's open acceptance comment requires one-second samples across at least 60 seconds, API health invalidation within a health tick, recovery, SQL-marker persistence, and clean deprovision. Both runs aligned the stop immediately after a fresh healthy Fleet report.

**Result: PASS on both OSes.** In 61 samples across the declared 60-second observation interval, PostgreSQL was inactive and its container absent. Rocky left `healthy/available` after 4.052 seconds; Fedora after 4.033 seconds. From the 5-second boundary onward, both had zero stale healthy/available samples. Both recovered PostgreSQL and the API, retained `r69-db-outage-persisted`, and deprovisioned successfully.
